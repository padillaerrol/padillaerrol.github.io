Excel · Workforce reporting

Monthly Team Scorecard

Tracks the team's monthly logged-case work and ranks each agent automatically, with a separate scorecard for the team lead.

Problem
Monthly stats had to be pulled together and ranked by hand, with no single view of how each person and the team lead were performing.
Built
A scorecard that combines logged cases, minutes worked, completion points, errors and attendance into a weighted overall score and ranks agents by role. The team lead is scored on team-level metrics like total errors and audit counts.
Result
Rankings update as monthly data comes in, with no manual sorting or scoring.

MS Access · Database design

Balance Sheet Database

Builds farm balance sheets for many clients and their entities, from data entry to a printed, consolidated report. Designed and built from scratch.

Problem
Each client farm's assets and liabilities were tracked separately, and building a consolidated balance sheet by hand was slow and error-prone.
Built
A relational Access database with 19 tables, about 100 queries, 20 forms and 30 reports. Forms update each farm's assets, liabilities and loans; queries group them into current and non-current categories and consolidate all of a client's entities.
Result
Any client's up-to-date consolidated balance sheet prints in a few clicks.

Python · SQL · Power BI

Job Market Dashboard

A data pipeline that collects job postings and turns them into a job-search dashboard.

Problem
Comparing Business Analyst and Data Analyst demand meant scrolling through job listings by hand.
Built
Python (Selenium and pandas) loads postings into PostgreSQL, SQL removes duplicates, and Power BI shows top hiring companies, locations and the best days to apply, with slicers, KPI cards and drill-down.
Result
About 2,200 postings in one dashboard instead of manual browsing.

n8n · OpenAI · Python

AI Video Automation

A workflow that turns a library of workforce-management tips into finished YouTube Shorts.

Problem
Making one or two short videos a day by hand (script, voice-over, subtitles, editing) took too long to keep up.
Built
n8n picks a hook, tip and call to action from a content library I wrote. OpenAI (GPT-4.1 mini) writes the script body under strict rules and returns clean JSON. Python generates the AI voice-over and subtitles, and background clips rotate by date. I review every video before upload.
Result
About 90 Shorts produced from February to April 2026, at one or two a day.
n8nOpenAI APIJavaScriptPython
